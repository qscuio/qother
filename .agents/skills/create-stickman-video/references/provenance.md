This is a selective adaptation of the director-before-generation workflow, style families, standalone character/voice locks, physically timed beats, format-aware composition and cut continuity. The qother contract/compiler/tests and generic queue story are independently written here. No upstream scripts were executed, no upstream videos or images are redistributed, and no provider installation or account setup was performed.

Differences are deliberate: preserve Chinese or any requested narration language; support arbitrary positive clip durations; no guaranteed retention rate; no mandatory controversy/CTA or visual-device quota; no forced music choice; no automatic cost claims; no silent ten-second rounding. Existing delegated continuation is respected with truthful evidence rather than a mandatory new approval stop. Actual media acceptance remains independent of prompt validation.
